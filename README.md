# Mechhanistic Interpretability Experiments
A single repository to keep all my learnings and experiments in Mechanistic Interpretability as I move forward.

### Premise
I am currently working as a Data Scientist in India and I keep on learning new things on the go, mostly related to machine learning and mathematics. I am beginning my journy of deep dive into mechanistic interpretability, or `mech interp` for short. This is a relatively new topic and very few research have been performed on it (again on a relative scale as compared to other topics in ML and AI). The fundamental idea of looking and interpreting LLMs (typically any model we call a black box as of now) on a fundamental scale and trying to figure out its inner workings is both pretty challenging and exciting to me. There is something very interesting about this topic that makes me want to go all the way into it. I will try to go as deep as I can into this exciting  (have used the word `exciting` two times as of now). Hopefully I will learn a lot of things in the journey, and maybe able to contribute something into it.
